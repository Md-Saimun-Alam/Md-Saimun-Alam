### Hi, I'm Md Saimun Alam

PhD student in Physics at the University of Toledo ([Biophotonics & AI Lab](https://www.rayresearchlab.com)). I combine optical microscopy and deep learning to build label-free imaging tools for cell analysis, drug screening and point-of-care diagnostics.

**Research**
- **CytoMorpheus** – label-free classification of cancer cell death from phase-contrast and dark-field time-lapse video ([code](https://github.com/Md-Saimun-Alam/CytoMorpheus))
- **FRAC** – deep-learning screening of sperm centriole integrity from multimodal microscopy
- **Modality translation and virtual staining** – phase contrast ↔ dark-field, label-free → fluorescence
- **On-chip microscopy** – lensless imaging with microlens arrays

**Tools**
Python · PyTorch · OpenCV · scikit-learn · Cellpose · YOLO · Grad-CAM · phase-contrast, dark-field and fluorescence microscopy
